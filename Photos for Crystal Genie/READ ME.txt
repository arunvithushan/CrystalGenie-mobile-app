Put gemstone photos here (JPG, PNG or WEBP).

While Crystal Genie is running (START_APP.cmd), every photo you put in this folder is copied
to the emulator within a few seconds. In the app tap Upload (or the gallery button on the
scan screen) and open the album "FromPC" - your photos are there.

You can also double-click CrystalGenie\tools\windows\SEND_PHOTOS_TO_APP.cmd to copy them right away.
Sub-folders are included. Removing a photo here does not remove it from the emulator.
